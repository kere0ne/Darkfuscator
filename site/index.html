What wraps your script

Bytecode VM

Your script is compiled to Darkfuscator's own VM bytecode and re-emitted as a loader. Nothing readable ships.

Layered encryption

Three layers: an add-cipher, an XOR stream and a rotation pass, keyed per build.

Heavy junk

Up to about 2 MB of dense dead statements across five junk levels. Levels 1 to 5.

Silent guards

Checksum and integrity guards fail closed, with no readable warning strings in the output.

Environment probes

29 checks: environment loggers, executor globals, string.dump sanity, math and clock consistency and more. The battery ships self-obfuscated through Darkfuscator itself.

Parse-verified

Every build is re-parsed byte-for-byte before it is handed back. A build that cannot re-parse reports failed with the real error.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Every feature, no tiers, no key system. Sign up with email and build.

Full preset with 10 VMs is slow

Ten VM layers plus heavy junk produce multi-MB outputs and take seconds to minutes. Lean builds (VM 5, junk 2) are much faster; the engine caps honestly when the payload outgrows the nesting.

My key stopped working

Keys can be revoked on the API keys page, and any build from a revoked key was never saved. Generate a new one.

Is this a security boundary?

No. Obfuscation makes scripts hard to read and change; it is not protection against a determined runtime attacker.
